Zhou Yi Xiao Liu Yao 周毅小六爻

A quiet study for classical Chinese divination and self-reflection. Cast three coins to receive a Book of Changes (I Ching, Zhou Yi) hexagram with moving lines, count an hourly Xiao Liu Ren reading by lunar month, day and double-hour, and browse all 64 hexagrams with the classical judgment and a plain-language note. Available in English and 中文.

铜钱摇卦 · Coin divination

三枚铜钱，六次抛掷，得本卦与变卦。Six tosses of three coins give a primary hexagram and a resulting hexagram.

时辰起课 · Xiao Liu Ren hourly reading

从大安起，依农历月、日、时辰数宫。Count from Da An by lunar month, day and double-hour.

卦例解析 · The 64 hexagrams

六十四卦卦辞、象辞与白话说明。Judgment, image and plain-language notes for all 64 hexagrams.

仅供传统文化体验与自我观察，不构成任何决策建议。For cultural experience and self-reflection only; not advice of any kind.
